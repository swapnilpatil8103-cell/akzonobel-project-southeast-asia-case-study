# Project Southeast Asia — Post-Announcement Addendum

**Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Status:** Update to a case study built before the deal was announced.

> Independent student analysis/reconstruction based on publicly available information. Not affiliated with, commissioned by, or representative of Goldman Sachs, AkzoNobel or Nippon Paint.

On 5 October 2026 AkzoNobel announced binding agreements to sell its Decorative Paints businesses in South East Asia to Nippon Paint. The rest of this project was written as of mid-September 2026, when the sale was still a reported process. This addendum records what was announced, compares it with what the case study assumed and concluded, and says which parts of the project are now out of date. The original workbooks, deck analysis and memorandum are left as the September record; they have **not** been re-modelled on the announced figures.

## 3. Valuation back-test

Applying the case study's own methods to the reported FY2025 figures (USD 291m revenue, USD 65m EBITDA) and comparing each with the announced USD 1.35bn:

| Method | Case study basis | Applied to reported FY2025 | Brackets USD 1,350m? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trading comps, EV/EBITDA (P25 / median / P75) | 10.3x / 15.2x / 26.2x | USD 668m / 991m / 1,700m | Yes (deal is 20.8x) |
| Trading comps, EV/Revenue (P25 / median / P75) | 1.81x / 2.83x / 4.11x | USD 527m / 824m / 1,196m | No — deal at 4.64x is above P75 |
| Precedent: SHW/Suvinil | 2.19x revenue | USD 637m | No |
| Precedent: Nippon/AkzoNobel rejected offer | 1.83x revenue | USD 533m | No |
| Precedent: JSW/AkzoNobel India (excluded as an outlier) | 25x EBITDA | USD 1,625m | Same order of magnitude; deal is 21x |
| DCF base / sensitivity range | EUR 613m / 468–908m | — | No — the top of the sensitivity range (EUR 908m) is 24% below EUR 1,200m |
| Recommended range | EUR 500–775m | — | No — the announced EUR 1,200m is 55% above the top of the range |

Observations, labelled as analysis and not as established fact:

1. **The recommended range missed on the low side.** The announced EV is 1.9x the mid-point (EUR 625m) and 1.55x the top of the range.
2. **The comps bracketed the price on an EBITDA basis; the recommendation did not.** The September reasoning treated the trading comps as directional upside context and anchored on the DCF. On the evidence of this deal, that was the wrong weighting. Precedents on a revenue basis were also too low.
3. **The EBITDA input explains only part of the gap.** Scaling the DCF by the ratio of reported to modelled FY2025 EBITDA (1.22x) moves the base case from about EUR 613m to about EUR 750m. That is a rough proportional adjustment, not a re-run of the model, but it leaves the price roughly 60% higher than that adjusted figure. The remainder is consistent with the buyer paying for expected margin improvement and synergies (Nippon Paint's own 2026 projection assumes a margin near 26%) and with a lower effective discount rate than the 8.88% used. The case study sized synergies only as illustrative percentages and did not feed them into the valuation.
4. **The excluded outlier was closer than assumed.** The 25x JSW/AkzoNobel India multiple was excluded as India-specific. The SEA deal priced at 21x, so that exclusion was not supported by this outcome. One data point cannot show the exclusion was wrong in general.
5. **Margin matters more than the revenue anchor.** The reported 22% margin is well above the 15.8% group proxy, so the business earned more per euro of revenue than the model assumed. That is why the EBITDA-based comps came closer than the revenue-based methods.

## 4. What held up and what did not

**Held up:** decorative-only scope inference; Nippon Paint as the strongest-fit buyer and the regulatory-risk flag; the method of keeping the trading comps and precedents visible as cross-checks; disclosure that the valuation rested on illustrative assumptions; the point that the three methods did not converge.

**Did not hold up:** the four-country perimeter; the EUR 300m revenue anchor; the 15.8% margin proxy; the DCF-anchored EUR 500–775m recommendation; the exclusion of the 25x precedent; SCG as a likely participant; "no signing or closing timeline" (now known).

## 6. Caveats

- Revenue, EBITDA, margins, the 2026 projection, the signing date and the brand and plant details are from a single trade article (SRC-039) and not independently confirmed.
- All web content was read through an AI page-fetch tool that summarises pages, not a verbatim read. The EV, multiple, proceeds, perimeter and timing were identical across the AkzoNobel release, the SEC filing and the trade article, which supports them.
- The back-test applies September multiples, from a 2026-09-18 data pull, to reported FY2025 figures. It is an after-the-fact check, not a re-valuation, and the deal price includes buyer-specific synergies that comps do not capture.
- No price or financial figure from a confidential process is claimed anywhere in this project.
