## A. Transaction Background

AkzoNobel N.V., the Dutch listed paints and coatings manufacturer, has reportedly engaged Goldman Sachs to explore a sale of its Southeast Asian decorative paints operations. The process reportedly follows public comments made by AkzoNobel CEO Greg Poux-Guillaume in April 2026 regarding a potential divestiture of certain decorative paints businesses in the region. Non-binding offers (NBOs) were reportedly expected by mid-September 2026.

Primary source: ION Analytics / Mergermarket, "AkzoNobel taps Goldman Sachs for sale of Southeast Asia paints assets" (accessed via WebFetch, 2026-09-17). This is a paywalled trade-press article; the content below reflects an AI-generated extraction of that page plus a corroborating web search, not a verbatim read of the full original text. Treat wording as paraphrase unless marked as a direct quote.

## B. Seller

AkzoNobel N.V. — market capitalization reported as approximately USD 12.51bn; company-wide 2025 revenue reported as approximately USD 11.47bn.

## C. Reported Adviser

Goldman Sachs — reported as AkzoNobel's sell-side adviser for this process. No information is publicly available (via this source) on Goldman Sachs' internal valuation, buyer outreach list, or process mechanics beyond what is stated below.

## D. Assets

Southeast Asian **decorative paints** operations. The article's language ties the reported revenue figure specifically to decorative paints; it does not describe a breakdown by protective, automotive, marine, wood or industrial coatings lines. Whether other AkzoNobel coatings businesses in the region (e.g., Performance Coatings) are included in the process is **UNAVAILABLE / NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED** from this source.

## E. Countries

Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam — reported as the geographic footprint of the assets under review.

## F. Reported Revenue

EUR 300m (approx. USD 346m) of annual revenue attributed to the Southeast Asian decorative paints businesses under review. No EBITDA, margin, or country-level revenue split is publicly disclosed in this source.

## G. Potential Buyers

- **Siam Cement Group (SCG)**, Thailand — specifically named as a possible bidder.
- Other global strategics named in market commentary as plausible buyers given sector overlap: Sherwin-Williams and PPG Industries (US); Nippon Paint and Kansai Paint (Japan); Asian Paints and Berger Paints (India).

These names are reported as commentary on plausible/likely bidders, not as confirmed participants in the process, except where the source specifically states preliminary discussions have occurred (SCG).

## H. Market Context

The ASEAN paints and coatings market is cited as growing from approximately USD 7.78bn (2026) to USD 9.92bn (2031), attributed to construction activity, infrastructure investment, and (per one summary) EV-related industrial investment in the region.

## I. Transaction Timing

- April 2026: CEO public comments regarding potential SEA divestiture.
- Process initiated following those comments.
- Mid-September 2026: reported deadline for non-binding offers.
- No signing, exclusivity, or closing date is publicly disclosed.

## J. Strategic Rationale

Reported as part of AkzoNobel's broader portfolio reshaping. Contextually relevant: AkzoNobel sold a majority stake in Akzo Nobel India to JSW Group for approximately USD 1.6bn, announced/completed around June 2025 — a separate transaction (India, not Southeast Asia) but indicative of a pattern of portfolio rationalization in Asian markets. This is background context, not part of the SEA transaction itself.

## K. Asset Structure / Sale-in-Parts

The source indicates the assets **may be sold in parts**, potentially to different local/regional players on a country-by-country basis where such a buyer offers the greatest synergies, rather than necessarily as a single combined regional platform. This is an important structuring consideration for the buyer analysis and process design phases.

## L. Information Not Publicly Disclosed

- EBITDA, margins, or any profitability metric for the SEA assets
- Country-level revenue split (Indonesia / Thailand / Malaysia / Vietnam)
- Legal entity structure of the assets to be sold
- Confirmed buyer shortlist or process participants beyond SCG
- Purchase price expectations, valuation range, or reserve price
- Confirmed exclusion/inclusion of non-decorative coatings lines
- Signing/closing timeline beyond the NBO deadline
- Whether Goldman Sachs has run, or plans to run, a two-stage auction process
