# Southeast Asia Asset Perimeter — Project Southeast Asia

**Status:** Phase 2 draft. This document maps AkzoNobel's known Southeast Asian operations across Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia and Vietnam. It does **not** establish the confirmed transaction perimeter — the ION Analytics article states only that decorative paints businesses generating ~EUR 300m revenue across these four countries are under review, with potential for a whole-region or country-by-country sale. Any mapping below to "likely included" vs "likely excluded" is an ANALYST INFERENCE, not a confirmed fact.

**A companion workbook (`SEA_Asset_Perimeter.xlsx`, to follow in the xlsx build step) will present this same data in the required CONFIRMED / PROBABLE / UNKNOWN tabular format.**

---

## Key perimeter judgment call

AkzoNobel operates two global segments: **Decorative Paints** and **Performance Coatings** (which includes automotive, marine, protective, wood, powder coatings). The ION article ties the reported EUR 300m figure specifically to "decorative paint operations." Several facilities identified below (e.g., Cikarang, Indonesia; parts of the Bac Ninh, Vietnam multi-site; several Thai sites) are Performance Coatings facilities, not Decorative Paints.

**ANALYST INFERENCE:** Based on the article's specific language, this project treats the transaction perimeter as **Decorative Paints only** in these four countries, and excludes Performance Coatings facilities from the carve-out financial model. This is a modeling choice made because it is the best-supported reading of the public disclosure — it is not itself a confirmed fact, and is flagged again in Phase 9 (Assumptions).

**Important limitation:** AkzoNobel's public segment reporting discloses Decorative Paints globally and a "South Asia Pacific" regional bucket that is broader than, and not equivalent to, the four-country SEA perimeter (Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam) referenced in the ION article. The reported EUR 300m figure for the SEA decorative assets **cannot be tied or reconciled** to a specific line in AkzoNobel's public segment disclosure with the sources gathered so far — it is sourced only to the ION Analytics article. This is flagged as a material limitation carried into the Financial Model and Valuation phases.

The primary FY2025 Annual Report PDF (`akzonobel-annual-report-2025.pdf`, linked by AkzoNobel IR) has been identified as a Tier 1 source but has not yet been directly parsed line-by-line in this research pass; a deeper pull of country/segment notes from that filing is recommended before the model is finalized (see Phase 9 open items).

## What Must Be Assumed / What Is Unknown

**UNKNOWN (requires explicit modeling assumption in Phase 3 onward):**
- Country-level revenue split of the EUR 300m total across Indonesia/Thailand/Malaysia/Vietnam
- Whether Malaysia's precise decorative manufacturing site/capacity is Shah Alam or elsewhere (not confirmed to a primary source in this pass)
- Whether "Eka," "Sikkens," and "International" brand presence noted in Indonesia sits inside or outside the decorative-only perimeter (Eka is a decorative-adjacent/wood-finishes brand and may be included; Sikkens and International are principally Performance Coatings brands)
- Employee counts specific to decorative operations by country
- Whether the transaction, if executed, would carve out only the legal entities above or include shared regional service functions

**Recommendation carried into Phase 3:** Build the financial model on the stated EUR 300m aggregate revenue base, with an explicitly labeled ILLUSTRATIVE ASSUMPTION for the country-level split (to be documented with rationale in `09_Assumptions/Support_Assumptions.xlsx`), rather than inventing site-level revenue figures that cannot be sourced.
